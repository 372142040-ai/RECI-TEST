What is Breakpoint?

Survive behind enemy lines.

Ghost Recon Breakpoint is a tactical open-world shooter set on the fictional Auroa archipelago. Nomad is stranded behind enemy lines while the Wolves, led by Cole D. Walker, hunt the Ghosts across the island.

This wiki is independent and fan-made. Every guide should separate official facts from player-tested workarounds and community advice.
