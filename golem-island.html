Project Titan

What Golem Island is

Golem Island is the setting for Project Titan, Breakpoint's raid content. It expands the game beyond regular Auroa missions with larger encounters, environmental pressure, and coordinated objectives.

What to prepare

  • Understand your squad role before entering.
  • Bring a stable loadout rather than a purely experimental build.
  • Read the encounter objective before moving to the next area.
  • Use the official Project Titan announcement as the source for launch-era requirements and feature scope.
Do not present abandoned or unreleased raid concepts as playable content. Keep this page limited to confirmed Project Titan information and clearly dated community updates.
