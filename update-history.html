Ghost Recon Breakpoint / Timeline

Read the roadmap, not rumors.

This page separates major content milestones from ongoing server support, with dated official sources instead of recycled update claims.

Update history

Major milestones

  • 2019: Breakpoint launched and began receiving post-launch fixes and content.
  • 2020: Ghost Experience introduced a more immersive way to play.
  • 2021: Ubisoft published a content roadmap and launched Operation Motherland.
  • April 2022: Ubisoft stated that the latest update was the final piece of new content, while server maintenance would continue.
Always attach a date to update claims. “Still supported” and “still receiving new content” are different statements.

How to use this timeline

Use the 2021 roadmap for planned content, the Ghost Experience article for a major system milestone, and Ubisoft's April 2022 statement for the final-new-content context.
